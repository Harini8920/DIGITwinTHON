Cross-Platform Requirements

One Digital Ecosystem

The eight modules should share common institutional infrastructure instead of functioning as isolated systems.

🔐 Single Sign-On

One login for authorized modules and services.

👤 Central Student Profile

Clubs, events, certificates, internships, placement, coding, achievements and academic information in one profile.

🛡️ Unified RBAC

Dynamic access control according to user role and permissions.

🔔 Central Notification Engine

Common notifications for events, placements, approvals, certificates and announcements.

📁 Document Repository

Controlled storage for certificates, resumes, internship documents and accreditation evidence.

🔎 Unified Search

Search authorized information across clubs, students, projects, achievements, internships and documents.

📊 Central Analytics

Institution-level analytics combining data from multiple modules.

🧾 Audit & Activity Logs

Track important operations such as uploads, approvals, edits and role changes.

📤 Data Export

Authorized export in PDF, Excel and CSV formats.

📱 Responsive Design

Effective experience across desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile.

🔒 Security

Authentication, authorization, validation, secure storage and document access controls.

🚀 Scalability

Allow future college modules without redesigning the complete architecture.
